Operating Playbook

How I Take AI from Ambiguity to Scale

Emerging AI capabilities from raw research primitives to trusted, measurable enterprise adoption — three principles that run every engagement.

01
Failure Telemetry First
Turn v0 Breakages into Eval Suites
Every production failure is a free eval. Instrument traces before you optimize outputs — the breakage pattern tells you where the schema contract is weak, where the tool boundary is leaking, and where the model is filling gaps it shouldn't.
02
Decouple Reasoning & Execution
Schema-Locked Tool Boundaries
Reliability in multi-agent systems is almost never a model problem. It's a contract problem. Schema-locked tool boundaries — versioned, validated, failing loudly at the edge — are what make deterministic execution real rather than aspirational.
03
Measurable Customer Trust
Ship With Clear Quality Bars
Customers don't adopt AI they can't explain to their board. Every deployment ships with clear quality bars — task completion rates, grounding accuracy, latency SLOs — and a telemetry layer that makes those numbers visible over time.
